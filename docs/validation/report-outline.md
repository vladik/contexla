# Assessment report outline

Every material conclusion should link to evidence or be labeled as an expert
observation, assumption, or unresolved unknown.

## 1. Executive decision summary

- decision being supported;
- assessment scope and date;
- most important conclusions;
- top risks and opportunities;
- recommended next actions;
- confidence and material evidence gaps.

## 2. Scope and method

- included and excluded systems;
- environments and collection dates;
- evidence sources;
- deterministic checks performed;
- interviews and expert review;
- limitations.

## 3. Estate overview

- installations, sites, environments, and ownership;
- technology and version summary;
- deployment and infrastructure topology;
- scale indicators.

## 4. Content and presentation model

- content types, fields, taxonomies, and relationships;
- templates, components, layouts, and usage;
- workflows, languages, and publishing patterns;
- unused, duplicated, stale, or high-complexity structures.

## 5. Integrations and dependencies

- integration inventory and data-flow direction;
- authentication and operational ownership;
- critical dependencies and single points of failure;
- undocumented or unverified integrations;
- target-state implications.

## 6. Custom code and platform extensions

- custom modules/packages and their purpose;
- ownership, supportability, and dependency risk;
- capabilities requiring migration, replacement, redesign, or retirement;
- areas requiring deeper code analysis.

## 7. Operations, governance, and quality

- deployment, monitoring, backup, and recovery observations;
- security and data-handling constraints;
- accessibility and governance indicators within scope;
- documentation and ownership gaps.

## 8. Findings

Each finding should include:

```text
identifier
title
category
severity or decision impact
affected scope
observation
evidence references
confidence
recommendation
owner or next decision
```

## 9. Disposition and modernization options

Classify relevant estate elements using an agreed vocabulary such as:

- retain;
- retire;
- consolidate;
- upgrade;
- replace;
- migrate;
- redesign;
- investigate.

Explain the evidence and decision consequence behind each important
classification.

## 10. Prioritized next actions

- actions required before estimating or procurement;
- quick risk reductions;
- deeper assessments or proofs;
- recommended sequencing and ownership;
- questions requiring customer decisions.

## Appendices

- detailed machine-readable inventory;
- integration register;
- evidence catalog and hashes where appropriate;
- rule/check results;
- glossary;
- exclusions and unresolved questions.
