# Paid pilot definition

## Objective

Prove that Digital Estate Assessment can reduce uncertainty for one real CMS
decision using approved evidence and a bounded, repeatable method.

## Recommended initial boundary

Choose one of these rather than an entire enterprise portfolio:

- one Drupal installation and its hosted sites;
- one Sitecore instance or selected site collection;
- one modernization program's highest-risk estate slice.

Record the decision the pilot supports, the environments included, the evidence
sources, and explicit exclusions.

## Included activities

1. Kickoff and decision framing.
2. Evidence availability and security review.
3. Customer-run snapshot collection, approved read-only access, or structured
   evidence upload.
4. Inventory normalization and source traceability.
5. Deterministic checks and architect-led analysis.
6. Review of material unknowns with customer specialists.
7. Draft findings review for factual correction.
8. Final report and decision workshop.

## Minimum outputs

- scoped estate inventory;
- integration and dependency register;
- custom code/module/package inventory when evidence permits;
- content-model and component summary when evidence permits;
- infrastructure and operational ownership view;
- risks, unknowns, and evidence gaps;
- disposition and next-action recommendations;
- supporting evidence references;
- reusable machine-readable export.

## Explicit exclusions unless separately scoped

- penetration testing or code-level security certification;
- formal WCAG compliance certification;
- complete migration mapping and execution;
- target-CMS selection or procurement management;
- guaranteed discovery of undocumented external systems;
- production changes;
- unrestricted collection of content containing personal or regulated data.

## Success criteria

At kickoff, the customer and assessor choose three to five measurable criteria,
for example:

- identify all known integrations and reveal material unknown ones;
- give estimators defensible quantities and complexity categories;
- support a retain/retire/replace/migrate decision;
- identify evidence gaps that must be resolved before procurement;
- replace or validate an untrusted inventory spreadsheet;
- reduce manual collection effort on the next assessment.

## Product learning captured during delivery

For every activity, record:

- input and source;
- manual steps and time spent;
- transformation or normalization performed;
- judgment required;
- deterministic rule that could be automated;
- evidence required to verify the conclusion;
- whether the step repeats across customers;
- sensitivity and deployment constraints.

The first platform vertical slice should automate the highest-value repeated
path, not every task performed in the pilot.
